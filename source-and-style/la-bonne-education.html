La Bonne Education (The Good Education)

Description

This print by Jacques Philippe Le Bas reproduces a painting by Jean Siméon Chardin and shows a mother patiently teaching her young child at home. It reflects 18th century ideas about learning at home, and as an engraving made after a painting, it was itself an early way of copying and sharing a cultural object with a wider audience.
